# Love Attachment Style Quiz — English Edition

## Purpose

This is the English-language edition of `qixi-love-quiz`. It gives English-speaking users an approachable view of attachment anxiety and attachment avoidance through 12 relationship scenarios.

## Audience

- English-speaking adults interested in relationship self-reflection
- Couples comparing communication and closeness preferences
- Social-media users who want a concise, shareable result card

## Assessment model

The quiz preserves the Chinese edition's scoring model:

- Six anxiety items: Q1, Q3, Q5, Q7, Q9, Q11
- Six avoidance items: Q2, Q4, Q6, Q8, Q10, Q12
- Four response levels scored from 1 to 4
- Each dimension is normalized from 6–24 points to 0–100%
- The two continuous scores map to eight descriptive profiles

The experience is inspired by the ECR-S framework but is intended for education and self-reflection, not clinical diagnosis.

## Result profiles

Steady Star, Warm Guardian, Grounded Pine, Blazing Tide, Devoted Dove, Island Glacier, Solo Falcon, and Thorned Aurora.

## Product behavior

The quiz runs entirely in the browser, offers back navigation, generates a five-dimension result summary, and can export a shareable result card with html2canvas.
