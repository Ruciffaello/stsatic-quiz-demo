# Love Attachment Style Quiz — English UI/UX Specification

## Visual direction

The English edition retains the original romantic cosmic theme so both editions remain recognizably part of one product family.

- Primary: `#ff4d6d`
- Background: plum and rose gradient
- Typography: Outfit and Plus Jakarta Sans, with system sans-serif fallbacks
- Tone: warm, reflective, inclusive, and non-clinical

## Responsive behavior

The interface is mobile-first and must remain usable in embedded browsers such as Instagram, LINE, WeChat, and Xiaohongshu WebViews. English text may wrap more aggressively than Chinese, so buttons and result sections must allow flexible height.

## Result card

The result card contains the profile name, attachment anxiety and avoidance values, five relationship dimensions, compatibility guidance, and a short reflection. Export uses a 2× html2canvas render with a dark opaque background.

## Localization rules

- Avoid gendered partner pronouns.
- Use plain international English.
- Keep profile names short enough for mobile cards.
- Preserve the original scoring values and thresholds exactly.
- Translate intent and emotional tone rather than Chinese syntax literally.
